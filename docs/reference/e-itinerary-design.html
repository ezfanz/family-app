E-Itinerary: design reference

20 screens exported from the design canvas. Each phone frame is 390 px wide and scrolls like the real app. Tap stop dots and cards, shopping checkboxes and amounts, theme cards, the ⋯ menu on Ideas, and Log out: they work. Links between screens jump to that frame. Colour and type tokens are at the top of the <style> block. Each screen's markup is in a <template> and its behaviour in the script right after it; a small renderer at the bottom draws them.
